# Paper Mono

Unmodified Paper Mono v1.000 files downloaded from the official release on 2026-10-22.

- Font: https://paper.design/mono
- Download: https://paper.design/mono/download
- Source: https://github.com/paper-design/paper-mono
- Archive: `paper-mono-v1.000`
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; see `v1.000/OFL.txt`.
- Archive SHA-256: `8642652985dc87bf4cfd07c89942899e611c3c774fc25284c0dd4fe86a993a13`

`v1.000/PaperMono-Variable.woff2` is the unmodified `fonts/webfonts/PaperMono[wght].woff2`
from the archive, renamed for its URL. Its weight axis runs from 100 through 800.
The static Regular, Medium and SemiBold TrueType files in `assets/og/paper-mono/`
come from the same archive and are used only to render the Open Graph image.
